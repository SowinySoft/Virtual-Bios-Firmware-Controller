\appendix
\section{Signed Image Header Format (256 Bytes)}
\label{app:signed_header}

The signed image header is placed at the beginning of each firmware image slot in the extension flash. It is verified by the arbiter firmware before activating the image.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{Detailed Signed Image Header Fields}
\label{tab:signed_header_detail}
\begin{tabular}{@{}clll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Offset} & \textbf{Size} & \textbf{Field} & \textbf{Description} \\
\midrule
0x000 & 4 B & \texttt{magic} & Magic number \texttt{0x56424649} (ASCII "VBFI"). Identifies valid header. \\
0x004 & 1 B & \texttt{hdr\_version} & Header format version (current = 1). \\
0x005 & 1 B & \texttt{sig\_alg} & Signature algorithm: 0x01 = HMAC-SHA256 (Phase A), 0x02 = ECDSA-P256 (Phase B). \\
0x006 & 2 B & \texttt{image\_version} & Monotonic image version (uint16, anti-rollback). Must be $>$ stored version. \\
0x008 & 4 B & \texttt{payload\_len} & Length of payload (firmware image) in bytes, excluding this 256-byte header. \\
0x00C & 32 B & \texttt{payload\_hash} & SHA-256 hash of the payload (firmware image only). Fast integrity check. \\
0x02C & 32 B & \texttt{pub\_key} & \textbf{Phase B:} ECDSA-P256 public key (compressed, 0x02/0x03 prefix + X coord). Reserved. \\
0x04C & 32 B & \texttt{hmac} & \textbf{Phase A:} HMAC-SHA256($K_m$, payload). Verified on boot. \\
0x06C & 32 B & \texttt{signature} & \textbf{Phase B:} ECDSA-P256 signature over \texttt{payload\_hash} (r || s, 32+32 bytes). Reserved. \\
0x08C & 116 B & \texttt{reserved} & Zero padding for future expansion. Must be zero. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\noindent\textbf{Verification Algorithm (Phase A):}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Read header at image slot offset.
    \item Check \texttt{magic == 0x56424649}.
    \item Check \texttt{hdr\_version == 1}.
    \item Check \texttt{sig\_alg == 0x01}.
    \item Check \texttt{image\_version > stored\_version} (in OTP/EEPROM).
    \item Compute \texttt{SHA-256(payload)} and compare with \texttt{payload\_hash}.
    \item Compute \texttt{HMAC-SHA256($K_m$, payload)} and compare with \texttt{hmac} (constant-time).
    \item If all pass: update \texttt{stored\_version = image\_version}, activate image.
    \item Else: reject image, fall back to original flash.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent\textbf{Constants:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \texttt{HEADER\_SIZE = 256}
    \item \texttt{MAGIC = 0x56424649}
    \item \texttt{CURRENT\_HDR\_VERSION = 1}
    \item \texttt{SIG\_ALG\_HMAC\_SHA256 = 0x01}
    \item \texttt{SIG\_ALG\_ECDSA\_P256 = 0x02}
\end{itemize}
